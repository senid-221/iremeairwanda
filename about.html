About IREME

Empowering Rwandan Education with Artificial Intelligence

IREME is a Rwanda-focused AI-powered education platform designed to support students, teachers, and schools across the country. Based in Kigali, we work to make quality education more accessible through intelligent technology aligned with Rwanda's official curriculum.

Our platform integrates with resources from MINEDUC, REB, and NESA to ensure that every learning tool, lesson, and assessment we provide meets official standards. Ireme AI, our intelligent assistant, supports both English and Kinyarwanda to serve learners across Rwanda.

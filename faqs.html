IREME is an AI-powered education platform built for Rwanda. It provides curriculum-aligned learning resources, personalized study support, AI tutoring, video lessons, quizzes, and tools for teachers and schools — all in one place.

IREME offers a free plan with basic learning resources. For full access to all features including AI tutoring, video lessons, and advanced analytics, you can upgrade to a Student or School plan. See our Pricing page for details.

Yes! All learning materials on IREME are aligned with the official Rwandan curriculum published by REB (Rwanda Basic Education Board) and NESA (National Examination and School Inspection Authority). We cover all education levels from Primary through Secondary.

IREME supports both English and Kinyarwanda. You can switch between languages at any time using the language selector in the navigation bar. Our AI assistant, video lessons, and learning materials are available in both languages.

Headteachers can register their school by creating a headteacher account and using the "Register School" feature. Once registered, you can add teachers, manage students, post events, and access school analytics. Visit the School Registration page to get started.

Yes! Parents have access to a dedicated dashboard where they can monitor their child's learning progress, view grades by subject, receive school news, contact teachers, and manage school fee payments. The school must link the parent account to the student.

The IREME AI Tutor uses artificial intelligence to help students understand difficult subjects. You can ask questions in English or Kinyarwanda, and the AI provides step-by-step explanations, practice questions, and study guides aligned with the Rwandan curriculum. It's like having a personal tutor available 24/7.

AI Video Lessons are educational videos generated by our AI system. You choose a topic, language, and duration, and IREME creates a custom video lesson with narration, visuals, and a quiz. These are great for visual learners and for revising specific topics before exams.

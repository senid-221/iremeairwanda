Can you explain how photosynthesis works?
Ireme AI

Great question! Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food using sunlight. Here is how it works step by step:

  1. Absorption of sunlight: Chlorophyll (the green pigment in leaves) captures light energy from the sun.
  2. Absorption of water: The plant's roots absorb water from the soil and transport it to the leaves.
  3. Intake of carbon dioxide: The plant takes in CO2 from the air through tiny openings called stomata on the leaf surface.
  4. Chemical reaction: Using the energy from sunlight, the plant converts water (H2O) and carbon dioxide (CO2) into glucose (sugar) and oxygen (O2).
  5. Release of oxygen: The oxygen produced is released into the air through the stomata, which is what we breathe!

The overall equation is: 6CO2 + 6H2O + Light Energy → C6H12O6 + 6O2

Source: REB Integrated Science P6 Textbook, Unit 3, Page 45

1. Introduction

IREME ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially students, teachers, and schools in Rwanda. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our platform.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email, phone number, role (student, teacher, parent, headteacher)
  • Educational Data: School affiliation, class level, subjects, academic performance, attendance records
  • Usage Data: Learning activity, quiz results, video lessons watched, time spent on platform
  • Communication Data: Messages sent through the platform, meeting participation
  • Payment Data: School fees payments, subscription information (processed securely through payment providers)

3. How We Use Data

We use collected data to:

  • Provide personalized learning experiences and recommendations
  • Track student progress and identify areas for improvement
  • Enable communication between teachers, students, and parents
  • Generate academic reports and analytics for schools
  • Improve our platform and educational content
  • Ensure platform security and prevent unauthorized access

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in these situations:

  • With Schools: Teachers and headteachers can access student data for their school
  • With Parents: Parents can access their child's academic data
  • Service Providers: Trusted partners who help us operate the platform (hosting, payments)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by Rwandan law or to protect rights and safety

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption, secure servers, and access controls. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Object to certain data processing
  • Export your data in a portable format

7. Children's Privacy

IREME is designed for educational use and serves students of all ages. For children under 13, we require parental consent before collecting personal information. Parents can review and manage their child's data through the platform.
